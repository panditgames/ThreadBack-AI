# ThreadBack AI — one-take video script (about 2:40)

Prepare a separate browser profile. Build and Load unpacked dist, pin the extension and open Dashboard. Close personal tabs and notifications. Demo Data replaces saved threads. Avoid personal sign-ins. If demonstrating live grouping, prepare two public pages with clearly related titles; otherwise label the synthetic demo and state that live collection was tested separately.

| Time | On-screen action | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Show several task-related tabs, then the popup. | “A browser can remember every page I visited and still forget what I was trying to finish. After an interruption, the hard part is recovering the task.” |
| 0:15–0:30 | Open Full Dashboard. | “ThreadBack AI turns browsing metadata into intent threads: a goal, related tabs, evidence and a suggested next step. It runs locally without an AI API key.” |
| 0:30–0:50 | Click Demo Data; point to three cards, search headphones, then clear search. | “These are three synthetic scenarios: a EurekaDev submission, headphone research and a physics assignment. Demo Mode lets you explore without collecting your own history.” |
| 0:50–1:05 | Point to task/domain groups; optionally show the prepared live task. | “During normal browsing, a Manifest V3 worker observes tab metadata. Local scoring combines titles, queries, timing, domains and tab relationships. Related work can cross websites; manual controls correct uncertain grouping.” |
| 1:05–1:25 | Open EurekaDev Timeline, close with Escape, then open Why. | “The timeline preserves the browsing sequence. Why shows retained signals behind grouping. These explanations describe evidence; they do not prove task completion.” |
| 1:25–1:45 | Open Graph, point to evidence and inferred milestones, then close. | “The Intent Graph organizes likely completed and remaining steps. I can inspect source links and decide whether the inference makes sense.” |
| 1:45–2:10 | Click Resume Task; return to the extension if a restored tab gains focus. Show the briefing. | “Resume reuses matching open tabs, reopens missing related pages and focuses the suggested next-action page. The briefing reminds me where I stopped. External demo URLs may require login; selected and successfully reopened tabs are reported separately.” |
| 2:10–2:25 | Close the briefing and show the card menu. | “I can rename, merge, split, complete, archive or delete threads. Saved context survives reloads and browser restart.” |
| 2:25–2:40 | Open Privacy Center; show disabled controls and exclusions. Do not erase during this take. | “Content extraction and remote AI are disabled. Metadata stays in local storage, with exclusions and deletion controls. Storage is not application-encrypted. ThreadBack helps me return to an intention, not just a URL.” |

Read only values actually displayed; do not invent percentages, counts or timing. Pause briefly after clicks. If external login redirects interrupt the take, keep the briefing visible and explain the limitation. Do not enter personal credentials.
